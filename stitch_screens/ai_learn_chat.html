How does caching work in a large scale application? Can you explain it simply?

smart_toy

At scale, caching is essential for reducing database load and speeding up response times. Think of it like a library.

auto_stories
lightbulb

The Library Analogy

Imagine a huge library (the Database). Fetching a specific book from the basement archives takes time. So, the librarian keeps the most frequently requested books on a small desk right at the entrance (the Cache).

When someone asks for a popular book, it's handed over instantly. If it's not on the desk (a Cache Miss), the librarian goes to the basement, gets the book, hands it to the user, and leaves a copy on the desk for the next person.
